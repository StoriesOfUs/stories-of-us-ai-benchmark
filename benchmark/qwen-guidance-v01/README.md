# Qwen evidence and guidance benchmark V01

This public-safe, manual-only benchmark compares one frozen Qwen3 4B runtime across three writing conditions using identical verified article facts.

- Condition A: Summary House Style V04 baseline.
- Condition B: V04 plus the owner's practical writing guidance.
- Condition C: V04, guidance and three original fictional style examples.

The protected 528-record corpus and its summaries are not included or used. Outputs are experimental, not editorially approved and not for publication. The three `fresh_unseen` cases were unused in earlier Option C outputs when frozen on 31 October 2026; the three `diagnostic` cases are prior development evidence.
